Refund Policy

Effective October 29, 2026

The short version

  • Build Camera is free right now, so there's nothing to refund yet.
  • If you buy through the Apple App Store, refunds go through Apple.
  • If you buy on buildcamera.com, you can cancel any time and get a full refund within 14 days of your first payment.

Free plan

Build Camera doesn't charge anything right now. If we add paid plans, this policy explains how refunds work.

Purchases through the Apple App Store

  • Apple handles billing and refunds for anything bought in the iPhone app.
  • To ask for a refund, go to reportaproblem.apple.com, sign in with your Apple ID, and choose the purchase.
  • To cancel a subscription, open the iPhone Settings app, tap your name, then Subscriptions.
  • We can't issue refunds for App Store purchases ourselves, but we're happy to help. Email support@buildcamera.com.

Purchases on buildcamera.com

  • First 14 days: if you're not happy, email us within 14 days of your first payment for a full refund.
  • Cancel any time: you can cancel a subscription whenever you want. It stays active until the end of the period you've paid for, and you won't be charged again.
  • After 14 days: we don't refund partial months or years, except where the law requires it, or if we charged you by mistake.
  • Billing mistakes: if you were charged twice or charged the wrong amount, tell us and we'll fix it and refund the difference.
  • Annual plans: if you cancel an annual plan within 30 days of a renewal charge, we'll refund that renewal.

When refunds don't apply

We may refuse a refund if an account was closed for breaking our Terms of Service, or if refunds are being requested to abuse the service.

How to ask

Email support@buildcamera.com from the email on your account, and tell us which purchase. Refunds go back to the original payment method, usually within 5 to 10 business days.

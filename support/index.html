Common questions

How do I add my crew?

Open a job, scroll to Job Team, and tap Invite by Text. Enter their name and phone number, then send the text. They tap the link, make an account, and the job opens. Only the Owner or Office can invite people.

How do I share photos with a customer or inspector?

Open the job and tap Guest Link, or select photos and tap Share. Anyone with the link can see those photos without an account. You can turn a link off any time in Job Info → Shared Links.

Why does a photo say "No GPS"?

Location was off or blocked when the photo was taken. On iPhone, go to Settings → Privacy & Security → Location Services and allow Build Camera While Using the App. Photos without GPS are stamped with the job's address instead.

Can I take photos with no signal?

Yes. Photos are saved on your phone and upload on their own when you're back online. Don't sign out until they finish uploading.

How do I zoom in the camera?

Tap 1×, 2× or 3× above the shutter, or pinch with two fingers. You can choose the starting zoom in Settings → Camera.

How do I mark up a photo?

Open the photo and tap the pencil. Draw arrows, circles, boxes or text, then tap Save Copy. Your original photo is always kept.

How do I delete my account?

Go to Settings → Account → Delete my account and type DELETE to confirm. If you're the only person on your team, the whole team and its photos are deleted too. If you're the only Owner of a team with other people, make someone else an Owner first.

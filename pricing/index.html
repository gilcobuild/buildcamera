Questions

Is there a free trial?

Yes. Every new company gets 14 days free on any plan. You won't be charged until the trial ends, and you can cancel before then from Settings → Plan & Storage. You can also use Build Camera on your own for free, with 1 user and 5 GB.

Who counts as a user?

Anyone who signs in to your company: you, your office, supers, crew and trade partners. Owners, customers and inspectors you send guest links to are free and don't count.

How much is 100 GB?

About 25,000 full-quality photos, at roughly 4 MB each. Settings → Photo Storage shows how much your company is using.

What happens if we go over our storage?

Nothing gets deleted and you can keep taking photos. We'll let you know so you can move up a plan.

Can we cancel any time?

Yes. Your plan stays active until the end of the period you've paid for. See our Refund Policy.

Do you have plans for bigger companies?

Yes. Email support@buildcamera.com for more than 15 users, extra storage, a signed agreement or invoiced billing.

What this is

This is the standard contract for companies that sign up a whole team, such as a general contractor rolling Build Camera out to its supers and trade partners. A company and Build Camera sign it together, along with an Order Form listing the plan, number of users, price and term. Email support@buildcamera.com to set one up.

This Master Services Agreement ("Agreement") is between Build Camera ("Provider"), and the company named on the Order Form ("Customer"). It takes effect on the date of the first Order Form signed by both parties ("Effective Date").

1. The service

  • 1.1 Provider will make Build Camera (the "Service") available to Customer and its authorized users during the term in each Order Form.
  • 1.2 Customer may let its employees, contractors and trade partners use the Service under Customer's account. Customer is responsible for their use and for keeping sign-ins secure.
  • 1.3 Provider may improve and change the Service, but won't materially reduce its core functions during a paid term.
  • 1.4 Provider will provide email support at support@buildcamera.com, aiming to respond within one business day.

2. Availability

  • 2.1 Provider will use commercially reasonable efforts to keep the Service available 99.5% of the time each month. This excludes planned maintenance announced in advance and events outside Provider's reasonable control, including outages at hosting providers, internet or cellular networks, or app stores.
  • 2.2 If availability falls below 99.5% in a month, Customer may request a credit of 10% of that month's fees, or 25% below 98%. Credits are Customer's only remedy for availability. They must be requested within 30 days.

3. Fees and payment

  • 3.1 Customer will pay the fees in each Order Form. Unless the Order Form says otherwise, fees are billed in advance and due within 30 days of invoice.
  • 3.2 Fees don't include taxes. Customer pays applicable sales, use and similar taxes, other than taxes on Provider's income.
  • 3.3 If an undisputed invoice is more than 30 days late, Provider may suspend the Service after 10 days' written notice.
  • 3.4 Provider may change prices for a renewal term with at least 60 days' notice before the renewal.

4. Customer data

  • 4.1 Customer owns all photos, files and information it or its users put into the Service ("Customer Data").
  • 4.2 Customer grants Provider a limited right to host, process and display Customer Data only to provide and support the Service.
  • 4.3 The Data Processing Agreement is part of this Agreement and governs personal information in Customer Data.
  • 4.4 Customer may export Customer Data at any time during the term and for 30 days after it ends. After that, Provider will delete it as described in the Data Processing Agreement.
  • 4.5 Provider may use anonymous, combined usage statistics that don't identify Customer or any person to run and improve the Service.

5. Responsibilities

  • 5.1 Customer will follow the acceptable use rules in the Terms of Service. Customer will also make sure it has the rights and permissions needed to photograph jobsites and share Customer Data, including any rules set by project owners.
  • 5.2 Provider will maintain the security measures described in the Data Processing Agreement.

6. Confidentiality

Each party will keep the other's non-public business information confidential. It will use that information only to perform this Agreement, protecting it with at least reasonable care. This doesn't cover information that is public, already known, independently developed, or received lawfully from someone else. A party may disclose information if the law requires it, after giving notice where allowed.

7. Intellectual property

Provider owns the Service, the Build Camera name and all related software and materials. Customer owns Customer Data. If Customer gives feedback, Provider may use it without obligation.

8. Warranties

  • 8.1 Provider warrants that the Service will work materially as described in its documentation during a paid term. If it doesn't, Provider will fix it. If Provider can't within 30 days, Customer may end the affected Order Form and get a refund of prepaid fees for the remaining term.
  • 8.2 Except as stated in this Agreement, the Service is provided "as is", and Provider disclaims all other warranties, including merchantability, fitness for a particular purpose and non-infringement. Photo stamps, locations and reports depend on users' devices, and Provider doesn't guarantee they will be accepted as evidence.

9. Indemnities

  • 9.1 By Provider. Provider will defend Customer against third-party claims that the Service infringes a U.S. patent, copyright or trademark, and pay resulting damages and costs finally awarded or agreed in settlement. This doesn't cover claims caused by Customer Data, by combinations with things Provider didn't supply, or by misuse.
  • 9.2 By Customer. Customer will defend Provider against third-party claims arising from Customer Data or from Customer's breaking the law or this Agreement. Customer will pay resulting damages and costs finally awarded or agreed in settlement.
  • 9.3 The party being defended must give prompt notice and reasonable cooperation, and let the other party control the defense. Neither party may settle a claim in a way that admits fault for the other party without its consent.

10. Limitation of liability

  • 10.1 Neither party is liable for indirect, incidental, special, consequential or punitive damages, or for lost profits, revenue or data, even if advised they were possible.
  • 10.2 Each party's total liability under this Agreement is limited to the fees Customer paid or owed in the 12 months before the event giving rise to the claim.
  • 10.3 These limits don't apply to a party's indemnity obligations, breach of confidentiality, or gross negligence or willful misconduct, or to Customer's obligation to pay fees.

11. Term and termination

  • 11.1 This Agreement lasts until all Order Forms have ended. Each Order Form renews for the same length of term unless either party gives notice of non-renewal at least 30 days before it ends.
  • 11.2 Either party may end this Agreement if the other materially breaches it and doesn't cure the breach within 30 days of written notice.
  • 11.3 When it ends, Customer's access stops, unpaid fees become due, and section 4.4 applies to Customer Data. Sections 4, 6, 7, 8.2, 9, 10, 12 and 13 survive.

12. Insurance

During the term, Provider will maintain commercially reasonable insurance for its business, including cyber liability coverage, and will provide a certificate of insurance on request.

13. General

  • 13.1 Governing law. This Agreement is governed by Louisiana law. The parties agree to the state and federal courts located in Louisiana.
  • 13.2 Notices. Notices must be in writing, sent by email with confirmation or by mail to the addresses on the Order Form. Provider's notice email is support@buildcamera.com.
  • 13.3 Assignment. Neither party may assign this Agreement without the other's consent, except to a successor in a merger or sale of the relevant business with notice.
  • 13.4 Force majeure. Neither party is liable for delays caused by events beyond its reasonable control (other than payment obligations).
  • 13.5 Independent parties. The parties are independent contractors.
  • 13.6 Order of terms. If documents conflict, the order is: the Order Form, the Data Processing Agreement (for personal data), this Agreement, then the Terms of Service.
  • 13.7 Entire agreement. This Agreement, its Order Forms and the Data Processing Agreement are the entire agreement on this subject. Changes must be in writing and signed by both parties. Customer's purchase order terms don't apply.

Build Camera

Privacy Policy

Effective October 28, 2026

The short version

  • Build Camera is a jobsite photo app for construction teams. We collect what's needed to run it: your account, the photos and notes your team adds, and where photos were taken.
  • We don't sell your information, show ads, or track you across other apps and websites.
  • Your team's photos are private to your team. Nobody else sees them unless someone on your team makes a share link.
  • You can delete your account and your data at any time.

This policy explains how Gilco Construction LLC ("we", "us") handles information in Build Camera, including the website at buildcamera.com and the Build Camera iPhone app (together, "Build Camera").

What we collect

Information you give us

  • Account details: your name, email address and password. Passwords are stored only in scrambled (hashed) form; we can't see them.
  • Company and team details: your company name, your role (Owner, Office, Super, Crew or Trade Partner), and the team members, trade partners and contacts your team adds, including names, company names and phone numbers.
  • Job information: job names, addresses, job numbers, customer names and phone numbers, checklists, tasks, punch items, reports and comments.
  • Photos, videos, voice notes and files you take or upload, and the notes, tags, work zones, trades and markups you add to them.
  • Your profile photo, if you add one.

Information collected when you use Build Camera

  • Location: when you take a photo, Build Camera uses your device's precise location to stamp GPS coordinates on the photo and to file it under the nearest job. Location is only used while you're using the camera, and only if you allow it. You can turn it off in your phone's settings, and photos will be stamped with the job's address instead.
  • Camera and microphone: used only when you take photos, record video or record a voice note.
  • Notification settings: if you turn on notifications, we store a technical address for your phone or browser so we can send you alerts, such as when you're mentioned or assigned a task.
  • Error reports: if the app runs into a problem, it may send us a short technical error message so we can fix it. These don't include your photos.
  • Basic technical information that any website or app receives, such as your IP address and device type, used to deliver and protect the service.

We don't use advertising trackers or analytics services that follow you around the internet.

How we use it

  • To run Build Camera: storing your photos, organizing them by job, stamping date, time and location, and showing them to your team.
  • To let your team work together: sharing photos with teammates, sending notifications and job invites.
  • To keep accounts safe: signing you in, stopping weak or leaked passwords, and preventing abuse.
  • To fix problems and improve the app.
  • To contact you about your account or important changes to Build Camera.

We don't sell your personal information and we don't use it for advertising.

Who can see your information

  • Your team. Photos and job information are shared with the people on your company's team. What each person can change or delete depends on their role, which the Owner or Office manages.
  • People you share with. If someone on your team makes a share link, anyone who has that link can see the photos it covers, without an account, until the link expires or is turned off. Guests can leave comments if the link allows it. If someone on your team texts photos or sends a job invite, that message goes through their own phone's Messages app.
  • Connected apps you choose. If your Owner or Office connects Build Camera to another app (for example through Zapier), details of new photos, tasks and share links are sent there. That app's own privacy policy then applies.
  • Service providers who run parts of Build Camera for us, only to provide the service:

  • When the law requires it, such as a valid court order, or to protect the safety of people or the service.
  • If the business changes hands, your information would move with it, under this same policy.

How we protect it

  • All information travels over encrypted connections (HTTPS).
  • Each team's data is locked so only that team's members can reach it, and team roles are enforced on our servers, not just in the app.
  • Photo files are kept private. The app uses links that stop working after a few hours, and share pages use links that stop working after one hour.
  • Two-step sign-in is supported, and passwords found in known data breaches are refused.

No system is perfectly secure, but we work to protect your information and will notify affected users if a breach happens, as the law requires.

How long we keep it

  • Photos and job information stay until someone on your team deletes them or the team is closed.
  • When you delete your account, we delete your account details within 30 days. Photos you took belong to your company's job records and stay with your team unless the Owner or Office deletes them.
  • Copies may remain in backups for up to 30 days before they're removed.

Your choices and rights

  • See and change your information in the app at any time.
  • Delete your account from Settings in the app, or by emailing us.
  • Turn off location, camera, microphone or notifications in your phone's settings at any time.
  • Ask us for a copy of your information, or to correct or delete it. Depending on where you live (for example California), you may have additional rights under the law. We won't treat you differently for using them.

Children

Build Camera is a work tool for adults and isn't meant for children under 13. We don't knowingly collect information from children under 13. If you believe a child has given us information, contact us and we'll delete it.

Changes to this policy

If we make important changes, we'll update the date above and let you know in the app or by email before the changes take effect.

Security at Build Camera

Your jobsite photos are your company's record of the work. Here's how we keep them private, safe and available.

Your company's data stays yours

  • Every company is walled off. Database rules on our servers lock each company's jobs, photos, notes and punch lists to that company. Another company can't see your data, even by guessing links or IDs.
  • Roles control who can do what. Owner, Office, Super, Crew and Trade Partner each get only what they need. Only an Owner or Office can invite people, manage billing or change roles, and those rules are checked on our servers, not just on the phone.
  • Photo files are private. Photos and videos are stored in private storage and opened through short-lived signed links, never public web addresses.
  • Sharing is your choice. Guest links for customers and inspectors show only the photos you pick. You can set them to expire or turn them off any time.

Encrypted everywhere

  • In transitEverything between the app and our servers uses HTTPS (TLS).
  • At restData and photos are stored encrypted (AES-256) in a U.S. data center (AWS, Virginia).
  • Daily backupsThe database is backed up every day.

Sign-in protection

  • Email confirmationEvery new account confirms its email, so no one can sign up as someone else.
  • Leaked-password checkPasswords found in known data breaches are refused, and every password must be at least 8 characters.
  • Bot and brute-force protectionSign-in, sign-up and password reset have attempt limits and Cloudflare's bot check.
  • Safe sessionsLogins expire and renew automatically, and signing out clears data saved on the device.

How the app is built

  • Locked-down website. A strict content security policy lets only Build Camera's own code run, which blocks injected scripts.
  • No secret keys in the app. Payment and email keys live only on our servers.
  • Payments by Stripe. Card numbers go straight to Stripe and never touch Build Camera.
  • Error monitoring without personal data. If something breaks, we get a technical error report with names, emails and photos removed.
  • Two-step sign-in for our admin accountsIt protects every account behind Build Camera: hosting, database, code, payments and the App Store.

Your control

  • Export anytime. Download photo reports as PDF, or export jobs for CMiC and spreadsheets.
  • Delete anytime. Settings → Account → Delete my account removes your account. If you're the last person on the team, it removes the team and its photos too.
  • No selling or ads. We don't sell your data or use your photos for advertising. See our Privacy Policy and, for business customers, our Data Processing Agreement.

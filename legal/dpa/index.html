Data Processing Agreement

Version 1.0 · Effective October 29, 2026

What this is

Companies that use Build Camera put their jobsite photos and information about their people and customers into it. This agreement explains how we handle that data on their behalf. It's part of our Terms of Service for every business customer, and it's also available signed. Email support@buildcamera.com to request a signed copy.

1. Who's who

  • Customer means the company that has a Build Camera team account. The Customer decides what data goes into Build Camera and why. In privacy-law terms, the Customer is the "controller" or "business".
  • We (Build Camera) process that data only to provide Build Camera to the Customer. In privacy-law terms, we're the "processor" or "service provider".
  • Customer Data means personal information in the Customer's account, such as names, emails and phone numbers of team members and contacts, photos (which may show people or property), locations, comments and job records.

2. What we do with Customer Data

  • We process Customer Data only to provide, secure and support Build Camera, and only on the Customer's instructions. These Terms, the Customer's settings, and how the Customer uses the app count as those instructions.
  • We don't sell or share Customer Data, use it for advertising, or combine it with other data for our own purposes.
  • If the law requires us to process data in another way, we'll tell the Customer first unless the law forbids it.

4. Our people

Only people who need access to run or support Build Camera can reach Customer Data, and they're bound to keep it confidential.

5. Security

We protect Customer Data with measures appropriate to the risk, including:

  • encryption in transit (HTTPS) and encryption at rest by our hosting provider;
  • each team's data locked to that team's members, with roles enforced on our servers;
  • private photo storage with short-lived links;
  • two-step sign-in and blocking of passwords found in known breaches;
  • limited administrative access, protected with two-step sign-in;
  • regular security reviews and testing of access rules.

7. Helping the Customer

  • People's requests: if someone asks to see, correct or delete their data, we'll pass it to the Customer and help the Customer respond. Most of this can be done right in the app.
  • Breaches: if we learn of a security breach affecting Customer Data, we'll notify the Customer without undue delay, and within 72 hours where possible. We'll share what we know and what we're doing about it.
  • Assessments: we'll give reasonable help with data protection assessments and regulator questions related to Build Camera.

8. Return and deletion

  • The Customer can export photos and reports at any time.
  • When a team is deleted, or within 30 days after the Customer's agreement ends, we delete Customer Data, except where the law requires us to keep it. Copies in backups are removed within 30 days after that.

9. Audits

Once a year, or after a breach, the Customer may ask in writing for information showing we comply with this agreement. We'll answer security questionnaires within reason. On-site audits need 30 days' notice, happen during business hours, are at the Customer's cost, and can't expose other customers' data.

10. Privacy laws

  • U.S. state privacy laws (such as California's CCPA/CPRA): we act as a "service provider" or "processor". We won't sell or share Customer Data, keep or use it outside our direct relationship with the Customer, or combine it with other data, except as those laws allow.
  • Outside the U.S.: Build Camera stores data in the United States. If a Customer is subject to laws such as the GDPR, contact us before uploading data about people in those regions, so we can put the required transfer terms in place.

11. Order of terms

If this agreement conflicts with the Terms of Service or a Master Services Agreement about personal data, this agreement controls. Limits of liability in the Terms or the Master Services Agreement apply to this agreement.
